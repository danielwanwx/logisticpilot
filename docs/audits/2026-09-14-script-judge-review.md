## Judgment

**The revised script is ready for a bounded rehearsal, not final narration or submission.** It now identifies the operator, the consequence of the exception, what Strands contributes, and why the result has business value. It is stronger than a sequence of business screens. Its most important remaining proof is a genuinely new agent-prepared twenty-unit proposal followed by the same-case approval and native readback. The old accepted case proves execution and proposal reuse, not fresh creation on the recording build.

## Changes required before final audio

1. **Accept the causal chain.** Use one new isolated case with 20 eligible / 5 held / 0 dispatched; capture actual tool selection, supported fresh preparation, one approval and external result. If this fails, revise to the completed-case backup rather than describe a new execution.
2. **Match verbs to footage.** “Chooses,” “prepares,” “confirms” and “shows” each need their actual corresponding event. In particular, a direct photo-analysis button does not establish agent tool choice, and a Delivery Note alone does not prove the five-unit hold.
3. **Accept the cut and access separately.** Generate final audio only after the rehearsal fixes the words. Then inspect full playback and the free judge test route; a script, local preview or past ERP acceptance does not complete either release gate.

## Why this pitch can persuade without fictional ROI

The useful before/after is a decision becoming completed ERP work: determine which stock can proceed, prepare that permitted operation, have a person confirm it, and verify the external result. Keeping twenty eligible units moving presents a credible revenue opportunity; it does not demonstrate earned revenue. Bringing evidence and preparation together presents a credible reduction in manual work; it does not establish minutes saved. The script states this distinction once at the outcome rather than interrupting every action with caveats.

The roughly fifteen-second architecture passage describes Strands reasoning and tools, application checks, a person and ERP without asserting an agent count. The contextual assistant's explicit preparation request can invoke the existing Strands economic selector and its four evidence tools; this is not Graph/Swarm orchestration. Historical Graph/Swarm or AgentCore experiments are not imported into the current runtime claim. This is a stronger technical story when the footage shows an evidence-based tool/action choice than when a diagram merely multiplies boxes.
