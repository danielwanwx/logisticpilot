# Winner tagline source notes

Assessed 2026-09-13 from the two official project pages and the official organizer announcement only. This is a description of what the sources emphasize; it does not infer why judges selected either project.

| Project | Exact page-tagline excerpt | Official award | User / job / outcome in plain language | Is monetary ROI prominent? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Province](https://devpost.com/software/province) | “An AI-native tax filing agent system that turns complex tax prep into a natural conversation by extracting data from W2 and 1099s…” | 3rd Place | US taxpayers converse with an agent and upload tax documents; the system extracts data, explains calculations, and fills Form 1040. The claimed outcome is a less confusing, more accessible filing process with 100% accuracy on its demonstrated Form 1040 fields. | **Secondary.** The page names $300–500+ CPA fees and affordability, but its headline and main proof center on task completion, accuracy, and speed rather than a calculated user saving or ROI. |
| [AgentShell](https://devpost.com/software/agent-shell) | “AgentShell redefines the Model Context Protocol (MCP) from ‘information retrieval’ to ‘body control,’ enabling a cloud-based AI agent to inhabit cameras…” | Best Strands SDK Implementation | Homes, small businesses, and people needing assistance invoke one cloud agent through inexpensive PTZ cameras; it sees, speaks, listens, and moves on demand. The claimed outcome is physical assistance or monitoring with less constant surveillance and lower hardware cost. | **Yes.** A dedicated impact section compares $20–40 consumer cameras with $500–2,000+ AI cameras, claims up to 50× lower cost, and gives a five-camera setup comparison. These are project claims, not independently validated ROI. |

The [official organizer announcement](https://aws-agent-hackathon.devpost.com/updates/38140-congratulations-to-the-winners-of-the-aws-ai-agent-global-hackathon) confirms Province as “3rd Place” and AgentShell as “Best Strands SDK Implementation.” Its Province summary stresses a natural conversation, document extraction, explained calculations, auto-filled IRS forms, and reduced barriers from professional-help cost and confusing software. It lists AgentShell's category award without a project summary.
