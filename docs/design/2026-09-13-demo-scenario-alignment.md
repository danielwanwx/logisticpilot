2. A fresh isolated scenario could begin at 38 received, 20 released but unshipped, 18 held and 2 missing. Completed PO20 must remain intact and cannot be relabeled as pending.
3. Existing whole-lot PASS processing automatically selects/prepares a contract allocation. Pre-staging this state does not by itself prove a new decision after the later failure.
4. Current Strands selection receives an application-compiled feasible plan. Comparing allocation results to that same rules compiler would not demonstrate incremental agent value.
5. Existing provisioning terms: A25 has earlier promise, partial dispatch allowed, minimum 10 and final-remainder exception; B15 has later promise, partial dispatch allowed, minimum 5 and final-remainder exception. Date precedes priority. With 20 usable, the current plan is A20/B0. Removing A's partial permission makes B15 eligible and leaves 5; it does not imply that all dispatch must stop.
6. Meaningful textual-contract interpretation or an investigation that changes the decision is new core scope requiring explicit design and acceptance, not something the existing demo already proves.
